# CLAUSE: one concept per clause (pre-registered)

MEANING (docs/MEANING.md) retrieves a concept from the bag of words of a *whole* story; COMBINE
(docs/COMBINE.md) composes pairs of those whole-story concepts and ranks the pairs by rank sum;
ROLES (docs/ROLES.md) read numbers but kept the whole-story retrieval. COMBINE and ROLES FAILed on
the same diagnosis: a story that chains two ideas is dominated, as a bag of words, by one of them, so
the other (often a generic one: "times", "boxes needed", "average") is not in the top ten and the
right pair is reached too late. CLAUSE reads the story clause by clause. Everything is behind
**`NOMAD_CLAUSE`, default OFF**. No neural nets, no embeddings, no language model: MEANING's counts
and search, WITNESS-gated.

## What is built

- `nomad/clause.py`, called from `world.evaluate` only after MEANING's stage misses and before
  COMBINE, only with NOMAD_MEANING and NOMAD_CLAUSE on, a field holding a lexicon, and **a story that
  contains numbers** (the clause binding needs them; a description without numbers costs nothing):
  1. **Split** at `. ? ! ; :` and at commas (a comma between two numbers is part of a list).
  2. **Number binding**: each number of a clause is linked to the input(s) holding that value on the
     first public row (a value two inputs share binds to both; same rule as ROLES).
  3. **One concept per clause**: each clause's own tokens retrieve concepts with MEANING's lexicon
     and scoring. A candidate inner concept A of clause i is ranked by its clause rank plus
     `CLAUSE_EXT` times the gap between its arity and the clause's quantities (or the previous
     numbered clause's: "the average of the three" names a concept after its numbers were given).
     The outer concept B (output type = the goal's) comes from any other clause.
  4. **Compose** `B[slot := A]` over clause pairs in order of `adjusted A rank + B rank`. A's
     variables range over the clause's inputs (plus the nearest numbered clauses' inputs when the
     clause has fewer than A's arity), B's other variables over its own clause's inputs (a clause
     without numbers: every input A does not use). COMBINE's exact interval inversion filters the
     inner instances; a public fit goes to WITNESS through `consult` (challenge rows only; a
     rejection costs 1 evaluation, at most 8). The stage spends at most `CLAUSE_BUDGET = 512`
     evaluations (every instance run, inversion probe and rejection is charged); on a miss the
     normal search runs unchanged on the rest.
  - Learns nothing. Empty field: no lexicon, no stage. Cold: never reads words. Off: never runs.
  - `NOMAD_CLAUSE_CONTROL=rewire` (control): same clauses and retrieval, but each clause is bound to
    the next numbered clause's inputs (wrong binding).
- `nomad/clause_tasks.py`: **8 sealed composites**, each `B[slot := A]` of two MEANING concepts, none
  a MEANING concept, none one of COMBINE's; **16 sealed families** (greenhouse, ferry, cannery, quarry,
  planetarium, clock shop, tannery, orchard: no other suite uses these), each story one idea per clause,
  one or two distractor quantities, inputs `qa..qg` shuffled, numbers of the first public row written out.
- `scripts/clause_suite.py` (seed **20270415**: unused; 20261120-20261231, 20270117, 20270203, 20270209,
  20270223, 20270307, 20270331 are taken), `scripts/clause_judge.py`, `tests/test_clause.py`;
  `switch_regress` += `clause`.

## Judge (`python3 scripts/clause_judge.py`, budget 10,000, max_size 7)

Field = `roles_judge.fields()` (16 WORLD families + easy science + the 72 single-concept stories; never a
composite). Arms (NOMAD_MEANING on, ROLES and COMBINE off, each task's story as `words`): `cold`;
`empty_off` / `empty_on`; `trained_off` (CLAUSE off); **`trained_on`** (scored); `shuffled_1..3` (lexicon
words reassigned to random concepts: same search, wrong concepts); `rewired` (wrong clause binding);
`combine_report` (report only: COMBINE on, CLAUSE off).

## Pre-registered criteria (committed before any sealed measurement; measured once)

All must hold to flip `NOMAD_CLAUSE` on by default:

1. **Clauses help**: `trained_on` certifies >= **+3** over `trained_off`.
2. **The concepts and the binding carry it**: `trained_on` >= best shuffled-lexicon control **+3** and
   >= `rewired` **+3**.
3. **Gains vanish with an empty field**: `empty_on` = `empty_off` exactly (certified, evaluations); cold untouched.
4. **No sealed task lost**: none certified by `trained_off` and not by `trained_on`; neither `trained_on`
   nor `empty_on` worse than cold.
5. **Zero per-task losses on every existing sealed suite**, switch on vs off, every arm
   (`SWITCH=NOMAD_CLAUSE scripts/switch_regress.py`, all judges but `clause`, including COMBINE, CEIL,
   MEANING, ROLES, MERGE, LET, LET2, INFLECT, TEXT_ABSTRACT): a loss is a task certified off but not on,
   or more evaluations on. Judges that pass no words are expected byte-identical; MEANING's descriptions
   hold no numbers (the stage returns at once); ROLES and COMBINE pass stories with numbers, where
   CLAUSE runs on MEANING misses (ROLES `rs_barrel_cellar`, `rs_star_plates`; COMBINE's misses). A CLAUSE
   miss adds up to 512 evaluations there and **fails this criterion**; the risk is known and is accepted
   rather than gated away after the fact.
6. **LOFO total 5,883, worst 1.0x**; the full test suite passes.

Decision rule: all six -> default ON in a separate commit; otherwise it stays OFF with an honest
write-up (docs + `results/clause/`).

## Dev (uncommitted, `/workspace/nomad/clause_dev`; 7 dev composites, bakery / library / farm; seeds 7 / 11,
11 and 10 hard)

Full judge: `trained_on` 7 / 7 vs `trained_off` 2 / 3, shuffled 2-4, rewired 2 / 3, empty 0-1 equal on/off,
cold 0, no task lost; `combine_report` 5 / 5. Chosen there: per-clause retrieval over MEANING's lexicon
trained on whole single-sentence stories; the arity-gap penalty (`CLAUSE_EXT` 6; 2 or 4 left product
behind volume in the first clause), `CLAUSE_POOL` 8, `CLAUSE_K` 3, budget 512 (1,024 found no more).
First versions found 0 of 11 when the stories used words the lexicon had never seen ("sold in boxes
of"): retrieval, not composition, was the bottleneck again, exactly as COMBINE's diagnosis said; the dev
and sealed stories therefore use the training stories' relational wording (`hold ... each`, `packed
into ... of`, `rows of`, `average`, `left over`), the same overlap-by-design as MEANING / COMBINE.

Honest framing, written before measuring: this is clause-wise retrieval plus search over pairs with an
exact arithmetic filter; it is not language understanding. The stories are templated English written by
the same agent as the training stories. The concepts that can be retrieved are those the field
certified (in 41 variants; the same function sits under up to five keys, which dilutes ranks). Expect
the sealed margin to be smaller than dev's; misses cost up to 512 evaluations.

## Verdict: FAIL (criterion 5). `NOMAD_CLAUSE` stays OFF.

Criteria 1-4 and 6 pass; criterion 5 fails on 32 evaluation increases (no certification lost) on COMBINE
and ROLES, caused by CLAUSE misses paying up to 512 evaluations before the normal search. Not gated away
after the fact. What the result does show: clause-wise retrieval is the bottleneck COMBINE and ROLES
diagnosed (sealed 2 -> 11, +9 over shuffled, +7 over rewired, about 65% fewer evaluations), and it even
certifies 4 more COMBINE-suite tasks. Next candidates, each needing its own new sealed suite: bind a
numberless "average of the ..." clause forward as well as back; and pay for CLAUSE only from savings
(e.g. charge a miss only when it is refunded by a later cheaper find), so a miss can never cost the
normal search evaluations.
