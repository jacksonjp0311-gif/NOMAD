# COMBINE: answering a story by composing two concepts (pre-registered)

MEANING (docs/MEANING.md) retrieves *one* certified concept from a story's words. A story that
chains two ideas ("two rooms of 5 by 6 and 3 by 6 tiles, sold in boxes of 8: how many boxes?")
matches no single concept. COMBINE composes two retrieved concepts: the output of one fills a
variable of the other, `B[slot := A]`, WITNESS-gated. The field never trains on a composite or a
two-concept story; only on the 72 single-concept MEANING stories. Everything is behind
**`NOMAD_COMBINE`, default OFF**. No neural nets, no language model: MEANING's counts plus search.

## What is built

- `nomad/combine.py`, called from `world.evaluate` only after MEANING's stage misses, only with
  NOMAD_MEANING and NOMAD_COMBINE on and a field with a lexicon:
  - the top `COMBINE_K = 10` concepts the words retrieve form compositions `B[slot := A]` (A = B
    allowed), ordered by the sum of the two retrieval ranks, at most as many variables as inputs;
  - **meet in the middle, goal-directed**: each instance of A over the task's inputs (MEANING's
    renamings, commutative dedup) is run once on the public rows (1 evaluation, cached); each
    binding of B's other variables (1 evaluation) is *inverted* along the path to the slot (add,
    sub, mul, floor-div by a known value; exact integer intervals) to the interval the slot value
    must lie in on every public row; only A instances inside every interval, on disjoint inputs,
    are composed and run (1 evaluation each). Non-invertible paths (mod, min, max, ite, a slot used
    twice, a divisor slot): every A instance is a candidate. The filter is exact (tested: it never
    excludes a true value);
  - a public fit goes to WITNESS (a rejection costs 1, at most 8); the stage spends at most
    `COMBINE_BUDGET = 1536` evaluations; on a miss the normal search runs unchanged on the rest.
  - Learns nothing. Empty field: no lexicon, no stage. Cold: never reads words. Off: never runs.
- `nomad/meaning_tasks.py`: `COMPOSITES` registry and `_concept` lookup (MEANING concepts unchanged).
- `nomad/combine_tasks.py`: 8 sealed composites, each `B[slot := A]` of two MEANING concepts and
  none a MEANING concept (boxes for two areas, leftover of a volume, mean-of-two times n, change after
  three purchases at one price, three legs there and back, groups-plus-loose minus two uses, average
  of a product and two counts, weekly change times weeks), independent Python truths; **16 sealed
  families** in six domains no other suite uses (lighthouse, apiary, pottery studio, bowling alley,
  space station, fire station), one or two distractors, inputs `ja..jg` shuffled, stories with the
  first public row's numbers.
- `scripts/combine_suite.py` (seed **20270223**, unused), `scripts/combine_judge.py`,
  `tests/test_combine.py`; `switch_regress` += `combine`.

Field = `roles_judge.fields()` (16 WORLD families + easy science + the 72 single-concept stories).
Arms (NOMAD_MEANING on, NOMAD_ROLES off): `cold`, `empty_off` / `empty_on`, `trained_off`
(COMBINE off), **`trained_on`** (COMBINE on; scored), `shuffled_1..3` (COMBINE on, the lexicon's
words reassigned to random concepts by `meaning.shuffled(seed)`: same search, wrong concepts).

## Pre-registered criteria (committed before any sealed measurement)

All must hold to flip `NOMAD_COMBINE` on by default:

1. **Composition helps**: `trained_on` certifies >= **+3** over `trained_off`.
2. **The retrieved concepts carry it**: `trained_on` >= best shuffled-concept control **+3**.
3. **Gains vanish with an empty field**: `empty_on` = `empty_off` exactly (certified, evaluations).
4. **No sealed task lost**: none certified by `trained_off` and not by `trained_on`; neither
   `trained_on` nor `empty_on` worse than cold.
5. **Zero per-task losses on every existing sealed suite**, switch on vs off, every arm
   (`SWITCH=NOMAD_COMBINE scripts/switch_regress.py`, all judges but `combine`; a loss is a task
   certified off but not on, or more evaluations on). Judges that pass no words are expected
   byte-identical. MEANING and ROLES pass words: there COMBINE runs on MEANING stage misses
   (MEANING sealed `ms_pitch_goal` and two easy-report misses; ROLES `rs_barrel_cellar`,
   `rs_star_plates`), so a COMBINE miss there adds evaluations and **fails this criterion**; that
   risk is known and accepted here rather than gated away after the fact.
6. **LOFO total 5,883, worst 1.0x**; the full test suite passes.

Decision rule: all six -> default ON in a separate commit; otherwise OFF with an honest write-up.

## Dev (uncommitted, `/workspace/nomad/combine_dev`; 7 dev composites disjoint from the sealed 8;
factory / hotel / camping; two seeds, 12 hard each)

Full judge, seeds 41 / 43: `trained_on` **9 / 9** vs `trained_off` 2 / 2 (PORT), shuffled 2-3,
empty 0 = 0, cold 0, no task lost. Chosen there: goal-directed inversion (a blind product of
renamings found 5 of 12 within budget; inversion 7-8), `COMBINE_K` 10 (5 / 6 / 8 found fewer: plain
"times" concepts rank low on story words), budget 1536 (1024 fewer, 2048 equal). Misses in dev:
compositions whose inner concept the words never retrieve in the top 10.

Honest framing, written before measuring: this is search over pairs of verified concepts that the
words retrieve, with an exact arithmetic filter; not language understanding of how the two ideas
connect (the story's structure is not parsed; the pairing is found by trying). Composites are
templated English by the same agent; relational words overlap with training by design.
