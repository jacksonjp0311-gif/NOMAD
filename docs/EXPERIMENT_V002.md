# NOMAD v0.2 Multi-Edit Experiment

## Question

Can execution-conditioned operator learning reduce bounded search cost for multi-edit repairs while preserving correctness on evidence withheld from repair search?

A second question tests composition: does the benefit persist on combinations of operator families withheld from multi-edit training?

## Policies

- **Fixed**: deterministic operator-family order.
- **Global**: learned operator success frequencies, no failure-state conditioning.
- **Contextual**: learned operator evidence conditioned on the current compact failure signature at every executed program state.

## Training

Each learned policy first receives the preserved v0.1 single-edit training experience.

It is then exposed to multi-edit templates 0–5. The contextual/global learner receives local transition evidence from executed candidates. A transition counts as locally useful when the candidate reduces the number of visible failing examples.

Templates 6–8 are withheld from multi-edit training.

## Evaluation

Five deterministic seeds: `11, 17, 23, 31, 47`.

For every test task:

- 9 examples are visible to repair search;
- 14 separate examples are withheld until the search reports a repair;
- max depth = 4;
- max executed candidates = 320;
- frontier cap = 512.

The hidden target source and benchmark composition label are never passed to NOMAD.

## Result

### Familiar multi-edit compositions

120 held-out tasks.

- Fixed: 120/120 challenge-correct, mean 39.32 expansions.
- Global: 120/120 challenge-correct, mean 39.35 expansions.
- Contextual: 120/120 challenge-correct, mean 24.90 expansions.
- Contextual reduction vs fixed: **36.67%**.

### Withheld operator compositions

60 held-out tasks.

- Fixed: 60/60 challenge-correct, mean 94.48 expansions.
- Global: 60/60 challenge-correct, mean 72.20 expansions.
- Contextual: 60/60 challenge-correct, mean 49.30 expansions.
- Contextual reduction vs fixed: **47.82%**.

## Negative result retained

The global learned prior was essentially neutral on familiar compositions (-0.08% mean expansion change versus fixed). Learning aggregate operator frequency is therefore insufficient to explain the contextual result.

## Failed design retained

An earlier v0.2 priority gave the learned prior enough weight to override stronger execution evidence. It sometimes reduced median search while lowering solve rate under a fixed budget. That design was rejected.

The released invariant makes an observed one-test improvement more valuable than the maximum possible learned-prior advantage.

## Claim boundary

This is an internal synthetic program-repair experiment. Withheld challenge examples improve the separation between repair-time evidence and final validation, but they are generated by the same benchmark harness and are not external replication.
