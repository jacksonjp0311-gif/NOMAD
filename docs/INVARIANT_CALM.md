# Invariant from CALM

Source: Tencent / Tsinghua, Continuous Autoregressive Language Models.
An autoencoder compresses text chunks into vectors. The model predicts the
next vector. Reported: 4x semantic bandwidth, 44% less training compute.

## Invariant

If a structure recurs across observed sequences, compress it into a reusable
representation. Predicting the representation is cheaper than predicting the
parts. The saving is measured in evaluations or compute, not claimed.

## Where NOMAD already has it

INVENT extracts common subexpressions from certified programs and promotes
them as primitives. Utility = uses x saving - cost. The field arm's probe
uses them as single nodes. The cold control never sees them. The sealed
judge recorded a gain on TRANSFER-3 (+4 certified) that failed one per-task
loss on science. Round 2 fixed the budget edge. The invariant is in the
code.

## What is missing

The prior is not causal for operators the system was not given. Abs, Min,
and FloorDiv are measured as reachable. They are not inside genesis. The
sealed comparison showed identical evaluations across cold and field. The
compression is computed. It does not change search order for new structure.

## Move

Promote a recurring structure from the field into the measurement path only.
Record whether the field certifies a held-out family with fewer evaluations
when the promoted structure is available. Do not wire into the continuous
loop until a sealed gain is recorded with zero per-task losses.

## What is not claimed

This does not produce general intelligence. It produces a measured answer to
whether compression of the field's own corpus reduces search cost on a
held-out family.
