# Plan v4 — close the operator gap

## Goal

Recursive field intelligence. Sparse binary packets are the field. Runners act, measure, and consolidate. The field updates only on a paired, WITNESS-supported, final-correct delta. No transformers. Bounded synthesis and field query. Not AGI.

## Gap

Absdiff, median, and clamp are reachable with Abs, Min, and FloorDiv. The sealed comparison showed identical results across cold, empty, and field because those operators are not inside genesis. The prior is not causal. The field is reinforcing science. The twin delta is small because the task is already solved.

## What is emergent

The field accumulates mass on domains it can already solve. The grammar is fixed. The field cannot bias toward operators it cannot express. Counts grow. Capability does not.

## Move

Put Abs, Min, and FloorDiv inside genesis behind a flag. Default OFF. When on, the enumerator can use them. The prior can change their order. A sealed comparison then measures whether the field certifies absdiff, median, and clamp with fewer evaluations than cold.

## Gate

Sealed suite, unused seed. Flag on and off. Cold and field arms. Zero per-task losses. At least one target where field evaluations are lower than cold. Do not wire into the continuous loop until that gain is recorded.

## What is not claimed

This does not produce general intelligence. It produces a measured answer to whether a prior can change search cost for operators the system was not given.
