# Plan v5 — abstraction promotion

## Goal

Recursive field intelligence. Sparse binary packets are the field. Runners act, measure, and consolidate. The field updates only on a paired, WITNESS-supported, final-correct delta. No transformers. Bounded synthesis and field query. Not AGI.

## Gap

The synthesizer is bounded to Add, Sub, Mult on one expression. Query answers by domain name or hash. invent.py extracts common subexpressions from certified programs. It is not wired into a measured loop. The field reinforces science. The prior is not causal for operators the system was not given.

## What the research shows

DreamCoder and Stitch grow a DSL by extracting common subexpressions from synthesized programs and promoting them as primitives. The invariant is compression: if a subexpression appears across certified programs, promote it. NOMAD has invent.py. It does not close the loop under sealed evidence.

## Move

1. Run invent.py on the current field. Record the promoted abstractions.
2. Add those abstractions as optional operators in a sealed comparison. Default OFF.
3. Measure whether the field certifies a held-out family with fewer evaluations when the promoted abstractions are available.
4. Do not wire into the continuous loop until a sealed gain is recorded with zero per-task losses.

## Gate

Sealed suite, unused seed. On/off for promoted abstractions. Cold and field arms. At least one family where field evaluations are lower than cold. No increase in incorrect certifications.

## What is not claimed

This does not produce general intelligence. It produces a measured answer to whether abstractions extracted from the field reduce search cost on a held-out family.
