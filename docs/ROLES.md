# ROLES: reading numbers and their roles from a story (pre-registered)

MEANING (docs/MEANING.md) taught NOMAD to pull up the right *concept* from a word problem, but it
still bound the concept's variables to the task's inputs blind: up to 360 renamings tried in
permutation order. ROLES reads the story instead. A story that writes its quantities out ("pays
with 43 dollars for 8 gas cans at 2 dollars each") is parsed for numbers; each number is linked to
the input holding that value on the first public row, and the words around it decide which role it
fills. Everything is behind **`NOMAD_ROLES`, default OFF**. No neural nets, no embeddings, no
language model: counts, learned only from WITNESS-verified receipts.

## What is built

- `nomad/roles.py`
  - **Mentions**: every number in the text with its features: up to 3 words each side (`L1:with`,
    `R1:dollar`, `R2:each`; punctuation stops the window; a neighbouring number is `#`) and the
    relational words of its clause (`C:each`, `C:per`, `C:left`, `C:shared`, `C:back`, ...; clauses
    split at punctuation and `and`). Words are lower-cased and plural-stripped like MEANING's.
  - **Learning** (`learn`, only from `WorldPrior.add_witnessed` with a new `row=` argument, only
    when NOMAD_MEANING and NOMAD_ROLES are on and the task carried words): for the certified concept
    (MEANING's key) each mention whose value matches exactly one input, and occurs once in the story,
    counts its features under the canonical variable that input fills:
    `concepts[key]["roles"][var][feature]`. Decays with the lexicon. Snapshots without role tables
    are unchanged.
  - **Use** (`ranked_maps`, from `meaning.stage`): every type-preserving renaming of a retrieved
    concept (up to `ROLES_POOL`) is scored by `sum_role fit(role, input bound to it)`, where `fit` is
    the log-odds of the mention's features under that role against all role mentions of all
    concepts (Laplace on frequencies, `ROLES_ALPHA = 0.05`; features never seen beside any role
    score 0; an input with no mention scores 0). The stage tries the best `ROLES_FIRST = 6`
    renamings of every retrieved concept first, then the rest in score order (each concept still at
    most `MEANING_MAPS`), with MEANING's unchanged budget (384), commutative dedup and WITNESS gate.
  - Switch off, words with no numbers, or a concept with no role table: the stage's plan is
    MEANING's, byte for byte (tested). Empty field: no lexicon, no stage. Cold control: never reads
    words. Final and challenge rows are never read here.
  - `shuffled(prior, seed)`: the control. Each concept's role table is re-pointed through a seeded
    random bijection over the concept's variables: same features, same counts, same lexicon; which
    role a word assigns is scrambled.
- `nomad/meaning_tasks.py`: `story_entry` / `train_story_entries` = MEANING's `entry` plus a `story`
  (the template written out with the first public row's numbers). `entry` is unchanged, so every
  MEANING suite, field and judge is byte-identical; a story has exactly MEANING's lexicon tokens
  (numbers are not tokens; tested).
- `nomad/roles_tasks.py`: **24 sealed families**, one per MEANING concept, in six domains no other
  suite uses (observatory, vineyard, circus, post office, ski resort, mine); every story adds two
  (14 families) or three (10 families) distractor quantities, often beside the same relational
  words (`with {x} friends`, `{y} meters`); inputs `ha..hg`, shuffled.
- `scripts/roles_suite.py` (seed **20270209**: unused; 20261120-20261231 and 20270117
  (TEXT_ABSTRACT) are taken), `scripts/roles_judge.py`, `tests/test_roles.py`.

## Judge (`python3 scripts/roles_judge.py`, budget 10,000, max_size 7)

Field `trained` = the 16 WORLD families + easy science + the 72 MEANING training families told as
stories (`train_story_entries`, 2 rounds, MEANING and ROLES on: the receipts fill the lexicon and the
role tables; training searches never read words). Lexicon 813 tokens / 41 concepts (= MEANING's).
Arms, every sealed task, its story as `words`:
`cold`, `empty_off` / `empty_on` (ROLES off / on), `meaning_off` (report: MEANING and ROLES off),
`trained_off` (MEANING on, ROLES off), **`trained_on`** (both on; scored), `shuffled_1..3`
(`roles.shuffled(seed 1/2/3)`).

## Pre-registered criteria (committed before any sealed measurement)

All must hold to flip `NOMAD_ROLES` on by default:

1. **Reading roles helps**: `trained_on` certifies >= **+3** over `trained_off`, or >= 0 more with
   >= **25% fewer** evaluations (total over the 24 tasks).
2. **The learned roles carry it**: the same bar against the **best of the three shuffled role
   controls**.
3. **Gains vanish with an empty field**: `empty_on` equals `empty_off` exactly (certified and
   evaluations).
4. **No sealed task lost**: no task certified by `trained_off` and not by `trained_on`; neither
   `trained_on` nor `empty_on` worse than cold (certified, or evaluations on a cold-certified task).
   Every per-task evaluation increase on vs off is reported.
5. **Zero per-task losses on every existing sealed suite**, switch on vs off, every arm:
   `SWITCH=NOMAD_ROLES scripts/switch_regress.py` over all 19 judges (learning deep / science incl.
   `sci_water_mol` / LOFO; transfer 1-4 and secondary; text; bridge; INVENT_TEXT; TRANSFER-5;
   TRANSFER-6; SELFEDIT2; TEXT_CHAIN; MEANING; INFLECT; LET; TEXT_ABSTRACT). None of them passes
   words with numbers, so the expectation is byte-identical records; the run verifies it.
6. **LOFO total 5,883, worst 1.0x** with the switch on; **the full test suite passes**.

Decision rule: all six pass -> default ON in a separate follow-up commit with the results;
otherwise it stays OFF with an honest write-up here.

## Dev (uncommitted, `/workspace/nomad/roles_dev`; factory / hotel / camping, inputs `ka..kg`)

Tuning only there: two suites (seed 31: 24 stories, 2 distractors; seed 37: the same plus a third).
Stage-only evaluations (a stage miss counted as +10,000), both suites: MEANING blind 64,357 / 42 of
48 hits; ROLES 10,497 / 47; shuffled roles 22,319-33,475 / 45-46. Chosen there: frequency-based
smoothing (raw decayed counts made the prior swamp the evidence), `ROLES_ALPHA` 0.5 -> 0.05,
`ROLES_FIRST` 6 (1 / 3 worse, 12 / 30 equal), the "MEANING's plan when nothing was read" rule (a
test caught the first-pass interleave reordering unread concepts). Seen in dev: the first-pass
interleave alone (shuffled roles) also saves trials when the right concept is ranked 2nd-4th, so
criterion 2 is the test that the learned role words matter. Expectation for the sealed suite: the
certified count rises where 7 inputs push the right renaming past MEANING's blind budget, and the
evaluation total falls by more than half; some tasks will cost a few more trials (commutative
concepts, distractors that share a role's words).

Honest framing, written before measuring: this is learned *slot filling* (which number goes where)
by local word context, over templated English the same agent wrote for training and sealed stories.
Relational words overlap by design; nouns, domains, names and distractors do not. Value collisions
on the first row make a mention ambiguous (skipped in learning, shared in use). It does not parse
sentence structure, and it can only fill roles of concepts the field already certified.
